# Modernité Interaction & Visual Design Audit

Grounding sources: Apple Vision Pro (editorial hierarchy), Tesla Solar (product narrative & dual-path actions), Nike By You (configurator feedback & spatial state attribution).

## Current Deficiencies
1. **Generic AI Layout:** Flat card outlines, repetitive spacing, lack of typographic scale contrast, and uniform beige background lacking architectural atmosphere.
2. **Missing Spatial Feedback:** When switching materials or surface tabs, the 3D model does not clearly highlight the targeted plane or display localized contextual tooltips.
3. **Hero Composition:** Static hero image without interactive hover micro-glances, architectural metadata callouts, or dual-intent entry ("Explore your building" vs "Talk to an architect").
4. **Step Navigation:** Rigid numbered buttons without progressive indicators, state badges, or clean architectural status indicators.
5. **Interactive Controls:** Generic sliders and inputs without tangible tactile feedback, sound/haptic cues, or polished visual response states.
