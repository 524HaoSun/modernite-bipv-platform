# SolarLLM interaction reference — 22 September 2026

Source: [SolarLLM](https://solarllm.app/), accessed 22 September 2026.

The public landing experience presents the product as a short, confidence-building sequence: **Trace → Model → Explain**. Its primary proposition is “Trace your roof, see your solar potential,” supported by satellite measurement, a small number of model inputs, an explained generation range, and clear planning—not guarantee—language. The Modernité redesign will use this as a structural reference: site/roof trace first, a concise model choice second, and an explainable estimate last. It will not copy SolarLLM assets or branding.
